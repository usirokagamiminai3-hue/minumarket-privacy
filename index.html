Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

MinuMarket eBay Manager is a seller management application operated by MinuMarket. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when an eBay account is connected to the application.

Information We Access

The application accesses only the eBay account information authorized by the user through eBay OAuth. At this stage, the application requests permission to access basic eBay account identity information.

OAuth Tokens

OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored securely and encrypted. They are used only to communicate with eBay APIs on behalf of the connected account.

How Information Is Used

Information obtained from eBay is used only to provide seller account management functionality within MinuMarket eBay Manager.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information or eBay account information to third parties. Information is not shared with third parties except where required to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, or protect the security of the application.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect stored account information and authentication credentials.

Data Retention and Account Disconnection

Authorization data may be retained while an eBay account remains connected to the application. When an account is disconnected, stored authorization credentials may be deleted or invalidated as appropriate.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the functionality of MinuMarket eBay Manager changes. The latest version will always be published on this page.
